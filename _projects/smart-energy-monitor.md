For ELECTENG 209 our team of four designed, simulated and built an energy monitor. It reads the instantaneous voltage, current, power and energy of an electrical load, using analogue circuitry and an ATmega328P.

We also added Wi-Fi, so the monitor can be programmed wirelessly and shows real-time graphs of the measured signals and waveforms.

The project was part of the Smart Energy Challenge. We were selected into the top four, judged by engineers from Datamars, and placed first overall.
