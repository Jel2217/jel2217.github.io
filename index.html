About

I got into electronics by playing around with small hobbyist electronics in high school, which led me down the rabbit hole of designing my own hardware. These days that mostly means PCB design in Altium, embedded firmware in C and C++, and power electronics, and I like working from datasheets and first principles.

I’m also a French horn player, so some of my projects end up making sound, or helping other musicians make it. Outside of engineering I like sailing and reading.
